Classification Logic

The ultrasonic sensor emits high-frequency sound waves (40 kHz) and measures the round-trip echo reflection time.

Detected (≤ Threshold)
Object surface reflected within the configured proximity boundary.
Clear (> Threshold)
Path unobstructed within threshold distance (up to 400 cm).
No Reading
Sensor timed out, disconnected, or physical obstacle out of bounds (<2cm or >400cm).

* Note: Acoustic distance monitoring detects reflective surfaces and does not claim biological person classification.

Hardware Architecture

Physical node hardware configuration for laboratory sensor deployment.

Microcontroller
ESP32-S3 (Dual Xtensa LX7 @ 240MHz)
Ultrasonic Sensor
HC-SR04 (40 kHz Acoustic Transducer)
Operating Voltage
ESP32-S3: 3.3V / HC-SR04: 5.0V
Echo Voltage Divider
1kΩ & 2kΩ (5V → 3.3V Step-down)
Effective Range
2.0 cm to 400.0 cm (±3mm accuracy)
Acoustic Speed Basis
343 m/s (0.0343 cm/µs in ambient air)
