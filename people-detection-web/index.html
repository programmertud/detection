Fire Alert Logic

The flame sensor detects infrared light emitted by open flames in the 760 nm to 1100 nm wavelength spectrum.

Fire Alert (Flame Detected)
Digital trigger DO active (LOW) or Analog flame intensity exceeds alert threshold. Emergency buzzer fires siren.
Safe (Normal Environment)
No open flame or anomalous infrared heat signature detected. Buzzer silent.
Warning (Elevated IR Level)
Moderate thermal source or sunlight interference approaching threshold.

* Sensor calibrated for rapid detection of candles, lighters, and open fires within 20cm – 100cm.
